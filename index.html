01 — About

Infrastructure is only useful when people can rely on it.

I'm an IT support specialist based in Mahikeng, North West, who moved from general IT support into school ICT and grew into work spanning identity, endpoint management, infrastructure and education technology.

I started at IIE Rosebank College's Pretoria campus, completing a Higher Certificate in IT Support Services in 2015, then returned as an ICT support volunteer. From there: St Patrick's CBC supporting a multi-campus device fleet and infrastructure, followed by my role as the sole on-site ICT Specialist at Kimberley Girls' High School.

Alongside employment, I founded EdiVision24 Technologies in 2019, providing IT and web consultancy to schools and SMEs.

IT isn't only configuring technology — it is users, reliability, documentation, security and staying calm when something breaks at the worst possible time.
