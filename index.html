01 — About

Infrastructure is only useful when people can rely on it.

I’m an ICT Specialist with 6+ years of hands-on experience across IT support, Microsoft 365, endpoint management, and infrastructure. My experience includes Entra ID, Intune, Active Directory, Windows Server, networking, and technical support across education and small-business environments.

At Kimberley Girls’ High School, I was the sole on-site ICT resource, supporting a 400-licence Microsoft 365 environment while managing identity, endpoints, infrastructure, and connectivity.

Previously, at St Patrick’s CBC, I supported a multi-campus IT environment spanning Windows, Apple, and network infrastructure, including JAMF Pro-managed devices.

I also run EdiVision24 Technologies, providing IT, Microsoft 365, and web services to schools and SMEs.

I’m now focused on IT Support, ICT, Systems Administration, Microsoft 365, and Endpoint Infrastructure opportunities, with a particular interest in building toward broader systems and infrastructure responsibilities.

IT isn’t only about configuring technology - it’s about users, reliability, documentation, security, and staying calm when things break at the worst possible time.
